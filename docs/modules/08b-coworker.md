# Module 8b — Explore Agentforce Co-Worker 🟡

**Phase:** 3 · Apply to Partner POC · **Goal:** ideation — how your forked Skill could surface through Co-Worker · **Time:** topic / no build · **Done when:** — (discussion only)

> 🟡 **Topic / discussion beat — nothing to deploy.**

Agentforce **Co-Worker** (GA) is the always-on, employee-facing agent surface. It's **likely already provisioned in your org** — confirm in **Setup → Agentforce**. There's no build in this module: it's an ideation prompt about where your work could land next.

The Skill you forked in Module 7 is a reusable capability. Co-Worker is one more surface it could reach — the same Skill, invoked from an employee's day-to-day agent rather than from Slack, the React Agent API, or the command line. A hands-on Co-Worker build isn't part of this kit yet, so we keep it as a topic to spark ideas for your own POC.

### Try it

Open Co-Worker in your org and ask it a few questions to get a feel for the surface. Then consider: **how could the Skill you forked in Module 7 surface through Co-Worker for your users?** What would trigger it, and what would a good employee-facing answer look like? Jot down one or two ideas to carry into your partner POC.
